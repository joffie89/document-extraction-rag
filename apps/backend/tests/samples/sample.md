# Workplace Safety Guide

This guide explains the basic controls used in a small workshop.

## Personal protective equipment

Wear safety glasses whenever machinery is operating. Use hearing protection in
areas marked with a noise warning.

### Inspection

Inspect protective equipment before every shift. Replace damaged equipment
instead of attempting a temporary repair.

## Emergency response

Stop work when an alarm sounds and follow the signed evacuation route. Report to
the assembly point so the supervisor can complete the attendance check.
